# EUODIA Printing Specifications

## Logo Dimensions for Bottles

### Room Mists (100ml)
- **Label Size**: 50mm (Width) x 70mm (Height)
- **Logo Size**: 35mm centered
- **Placement**: 25mm from the bottom of the bottle

### Reed Diffusers (150ml)
- **Label Size**: 60mm (Width) x 80mm (Height)
- **Logo Size**: 40mm centered
- **Placement**: 30mm from the bottom of the bottle

## Logo Variations Included
1. **EUODIA_LOGO_GOLD.png**: Original gold luxury finish.
2. **EUODIA_LOGO_BLACK.png**: High-contrast black for light backgrounds.
3. **EUODIA_LOGO_WHITE.png**: Clean white for dark frosting or colored glass.

## Printing Notes
- **Material**: Clear or Frosted Vinyl suggested for 'Direct to Glass' look.
- **Finish**: Metallic Foil or High-Build Spot UV for the Gold Logo to maintain the premium feel.
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI (Provided assets are high-resolution).
